Common questions

Things people usually ask us

What's your typical project timeline?

A focused MVP typically takes 12–16 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger or more complex builds vary — we'll give you a realistic estimate after discovery, not before.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes, and we enjoy it. We have experience taking products from zero to launch and understand the constraints of early-stage funding and the need to validate quickly.

What happens when the project ends?

You own everything — all code, infrastructure, and intellectual property. We'll hand over a fully documented codebase and can remain engaged on a retainer if that's useful.

How do you handle changes in scope?

We discuss scope changes openly before doing any work. Nothing is ever added to the bill without your explicit sign-off first.

Do you work remotely?

Yes — our team is fully distributed and we've been remote-first since day one. We're experienced at async collaboration and timezone-distributed projects.

What's your minimum engagement size?

Typically £20,000 for a fixed-scope project or £8,000/month on a retainer basis. Discovery sprints start at £5,000 for teams who want to begin more cautiously.
